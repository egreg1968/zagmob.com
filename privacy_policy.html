Out Lived! Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

App: Out Lived! (ZaGMob LLC) • Bundle ID: com.zagmob.outlived

Out Lived! (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles, what leaves your device, and what choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

The following stay on your phone or tablet unless you uninstall the app:

  • Display name and birthdate (for age calculations)
  • Goal age, streak, favorites, and app preferences
  • Premium entitlement flags and gift redemption state
  • The bundled historical-figures database (Pantheon dataset)

We do not upload your birthdate to our servers.

2. Location

If you tap Near Me on the map and grant permission, the app reads your precise location to center the map and show nearby figures. That location is used on-device for that session and is not sent to ZaGMob servers.

3. Gift codes and anonymous accounts

Lifetime premium users can create gift codes. That flow creates an anonymous Firebase account on your device so we can enforce monthly gift limits. We may store:

  • Anonymous user ID (Firebase Auth)
  • Optional display name (from your on-device name, if you gift)
  • Gift code metadata (creation time, redemption status)

You can remove this cloud data anytime: Settings → Delete cloud account. That deletes your anonymous account and associated gift records on our servers. On-device app data is not removed.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

In release builds we use Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — pseudonymous usage events (e.g. tab views, shares, premium prompts). We do not send your name or birthdate in analytics parameters.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash logs and diagnostics to fix stability issues.

Debug and profile builds disable analytics and crash upload by default.

5. Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, app interaction data, and approximate location (e.g. from IP) for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement.

Where required, we show Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. You can manage ad personalization in device settings and, on supported devices, through ad privacy options in the app where available.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (and Apple Advertising preferences)
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Purchases

Lifetime premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment and purchase history are handled by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies. We receive only what is needed to unlock premium features on your device.

7. Other third-party services

  • Firebase Remote Config — feature flags (no personal data).
  • Wikimedia Commons — portrait images are fetched directly from Wikimedia when you view or share figures; those requests go to Wikimedia, not ZaGMob.
  • Amazon affiliate links (optional) — if enabled, links may include our affiliate tag; Amazon’s policies apply when you visit their site.

8. Data retention and security

On-device data remains until you delete the app or clear app storage. Cloud gift data is deleted when you use Delete cloud account. Analytics and crash data are retained per Google Firebase default retention policies.

Data transmitted to our backends and Google services uses HTTPS/TLS.

9. Children’s privacy

Out Lived! is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
