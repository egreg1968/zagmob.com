Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 27, 2025

Outlived ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from you. The birthdate you enter is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob (operated by Google) may collect certain data automatically for the purpose of serving personalized ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. This may include:

  • Device advertising ID
  • Approximate location (inferred from IP address)
  • Device information and app usage data

We do not control AdMob's data practices. For details on how Google uses this information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

3. Data Storage and Security

All data entered in the App (such as your birthdate) is stored locally on your device using secure, standard mobile storage practices. We have no access to it.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
