Frequently Asked Questions

What is Out Lived!?

Out Lived! is a mindful daily app that shows you up to 5 real historical figures from the Pantheon dataset who lived rich lives but died exactly one day short of your current age. It's a quiet reminder that time is precious.

Is my data private?

Yes — completely. Enter your birthdate once. Everything stays on your device in local SQLite. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud. Built with Flutter for a clean, private experience.

How accurate is the age matching?

Very accurate. We use verified historical data and calculate your exact age in days right now. The app quietly compares it to thousands of notable figures.

Why a one-time payment instead of subscription?

We hate subscription fatigue too. Premium is a simple $2.99 one-time upgrade that unlocks all features and removes ads for life.
