Out Lived!

Every day the app shows you up to 5 real historical figures who lived rich lives — and stopped exactly one day short of where you are right now.

A gentle daily reminder that every sunrise is overtime

📅 One-time setup

Enter your birthdate once. The app does the rest — no accounts, no tracking.

🧠 Powered by real history

Uses the Pantheon dataset — thousands of verified historical figures.

📱 Clean & private

Built with Flutter. All data lives offline in SQLite.

How it works

Today you are X days old.
Out Lived! quietly shows you notable people who reached the same age… and stopped there.

Why we built Out Lived!

Two longtime friends (one 30-year IT veteran named Greg, one electrical engineer named Zak) wanted a daily app worth opening — thoughtful, private, and slightly philosophical. No doom-scrolling. Just a quiet reminder that time is precious and you're already ahead of some legends.
