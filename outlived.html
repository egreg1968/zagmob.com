Frequently Asked Questions

What is Out Lived!?

Out Lived! is a mindful daily app that shows you real historical figures from the Pantheon dataset who lived rich lives but died exactly one day short of your current age. Version 2.0 keeps that daily spark and adds share cards, a home widget, Hall of Fame bookmarks, birthday mode, and more.

Is my data private?

Yes — private by default. Your birthdate and core progress stay on your device. Analytics and ads (free tier) use industry-standard providers; we don’t send your birthdate to our servers. Gifting Premium creates an optional anonymous cloud account so gift limits work — you can remove it anytime under Settings → Delete cloud account. See our full Privacy Policy.

How accurate is the age matching?

Very accurate. We use verified historical data and calculate your exact age in days right now. The app quietly compares it to thousands of notable figures.

What is Birthday mode?

On your birthday, the app switches into a special mode to celebrate another year of overtime — a small ritual for the day that started it all.

Why a one-time payment instead of subscription?

We hate subscription fatigue too. Premium is a simple one-time upgrade that unlocks all features and removes ads for life. You can also gift or redeem a free Premium week.
